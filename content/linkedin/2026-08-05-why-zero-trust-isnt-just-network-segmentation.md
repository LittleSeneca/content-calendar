# Why Zero Trust Isn't Just Network Segmentation

Most people hear "Zero Trust" and think microsegmentation. That's maybe 20% of the picture.

The real shift is **identity-first**. Every access decision starts with "who are you and what should you see," not "what network are you on."

## Three things most orgs miss:

1. **Device posture matters more than IP address.** A managed device from a coffee shop should be treated differently than an unmanaged device on the corporate LAN.
2. **Continuous verification beats one-time auth.** Just because you passed MFA at 9am doesn't mean your session should be trusted at 3pm.
3. **Data classification is the foundation.** You can't protect what you haven't labeled.

The network perimeter died years ago. Stop building walls and start verifying every request.
