# SOC 2 Type II: What Actually Matters in Year One

I've been through this rodeo enough times to know: the first SOC 2 is always a mess. Here's what I'd do differently.

## Skip the tool shopping spree

New SOC 2 vendors love selling you a platform. Drata, Vanta, Secureframe — they're all fine. But buying one before you understand your controls is like buying a gym membership before you know what exercises you need.

## Start with these five controls

1. **Access reviews** — quarterly, documented, boring
2. **Change management** — peer review + approval trail
3. **Vendor risk** — spreadsheet first, tool later
4. **Background checks** — just do them
5. **Incident response** — write the runbook before you need it

Everything else builds from these. Get these right and the auditor stops digging.
